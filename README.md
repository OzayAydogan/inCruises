InCruises Business Hub

Traveling the world is no longer a distant dream — it’s becoming a real, reachable lifestyle for everyday people. This repository is built to support that mission. It organizes all the tools, scripts, training materials, and resources needed to grow a successful InCruises business while helping more people experience global travel in a simple, affordable, and meaningful way.

🌍 About InCruises
InCruises is a global membership club that makes travel more accessible through exclusive cruise deals, transparent pricing, and a unique rewards system. Members can explore the world while saving money, building unforgettable memories, and joining a worldwide community of travelers.

For those who choose the Partner model, InCruises also offers a powerful business opportunity. By sharing the membership with others, Partners can earn income, develop leadership skills, and build a scalable global team. It’s a chance to grow personally, professionally, and financially — all while helping others travel more.
